## Portugal (PT_PT / PT_BR)

### Promotional text
Live CP departures, trip countdown, and a Home Screen widget for Portugal stations in your pocket.

### Description
Meet VeryStays, your train companion for Portugal. Find stations fast, check live CP departures and arrivals, and keep your next train countdown visible on your Home Screen.

What you can do:

- Explore 500+ stations across Portugal (plus Spanish hubs in the same guide)
- Check live departures (and next arrivals) at CP stations
- Tap Take to track the train you're catching, or Meet for a terminating arrival
- Trip countdown with delay updates and upcoming stops
- Home-screen widget for at-a-glance updates
- Live Activity on Lock Screen and Dynamic Island (iPhone)
- On-time reliability scores and a colour-coded station map
- Airport connection maps, nearby hotel ideas, and a tickets guide

Built for quick, confident travel (commuting or long-distance) across Iberia.
