## Full description

```
VeryStays puts Portugal and Spain’s train hubs in your pocket — live boards, trip countdowns, and nearby stays for the stations that matter.

FIND YOUR STATION
Browse hundreds of hubs across Portugal and Spain. Search by name, sort by distance, and open a station page with a photo, summary, lines, and practical next steps.

LIVE DEPARTURES & ARRIVALS
See upcoming trains at CP stations (and more). Tap Take on a departure — or on an arrival that continues — to start a countdown for the train you’re catching. Tap Meet on a train that terminates here to track its arrival without adding it to your trip history.

TRIP COUNTDOWN
Keep your next leave time front and centre. Track delay updates while you wait, see upcoming stops when available, and glance at past trains you’ve taken.

ON-TIME RELIABILITY
Check a station’s punctuality score based on sampled live boards — useful when you’re choosing where to wait or where to stay.

MAP & RANKINGS
Explore stations on the map, colour-coded by reliability. Compare community favourites and on-time rankings.

AIRPORTS & HOTELS
Airport hubs show collected flight-connection maps. Many stations include budget-friendly hotel ideas with Booking search links nearby.

TICKETS GUIDE
A clear, offline-friendly guide to buying train and metro tickets in Portugal and Spain — with links to official apps and operators.

OPTIONAL LOCATION & REMINDERS
Allow location to sort stations near you. Turn on notifications for a gentle nudge before your tracked train leaves.

Made for travellers who move by rail across the Iberian Peninsula — from urban hops to long-distance journeys.

Website: https://www.verystays.com
```
