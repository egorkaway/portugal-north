## Full description

```
Meet VeryStays, your train companion for Portugal and Spain. Find stations fast, check live Portuguese CP departures and arrivals, and keep your next train countdown visible on your Home Screen.

What you can do:

- Explore 1,400+ stations across the Iberian Peninsula
- Check live departures (and next arrivals) at Portuguese CP stations
- Tap Take to track the train you're catching, or Meet for a terminating arrival
- Trip countdown with delay updates and upcoming stops
- Home-screen widget for at-a-glance updates
- Live Activity on Lock Screen and Dynamic Island (iPhone)
- On-time reliability scores and a colour-coded station map for Portugal and Spain
- Airport connection maps, nearby hotel ideas, and a tickets guide

Built for quick, confident travel (commuting or long-distance) across Iberia.

Privacy Policy: https://egor.one/privacy
Website: https://www.verystays.com/
```
