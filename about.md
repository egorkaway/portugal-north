# About VeryStays

Devpost / project About copy.

## Inspiration

Train info across Portugal and Spain is scattered across boards, apps, and maps. We wanted one place to find a station, see what’s leaving next, and know if it’s worth trusting whether you’re commuting or connecting cross-border.

## What it does

VeryStays is a train companion for Iberia: 400+ stations, live departures/arrivals, trip tracking with countdown (plus iOS/Android widget & iOS Live Activity), a reliability map, rankings, nearby stays, and a tickets guide.

## How we built it

A Vite/React PWA on Vercel plus a native Expo iOS app. Curated station data in-repo; live boards and journeys from CP where available; reliability from departure stats; votes via serverless APIs. Mobile adds widgets and Live Activities App Store release tooling.

## Challenges we ran into

Uneven live data between Portugal and Spain mostly.

## Accomplishments that we're proud of

It is first of our vibe coded apps with proper test coverage in the code.

## What's next for VeryStays

Better Spain coverage, what we know and show for Portugal is way more complete so far.
